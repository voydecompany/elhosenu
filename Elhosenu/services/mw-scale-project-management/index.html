ENGINEERING PROFILE

PRECISION-DRIVEN METHODOLOGY.

Multi-megawatt solar projects involve complex timelines, multiple contractors and stringent regulatory milestones. ElHosenu delivers engineering-focused project management that safeguards quality, schedule and financial viability.

As Owner's Engineers or Technical Advisors, we orchestrate the interaction between civil, electrical, structural, geotechnical and interconnection teams. We monitor deliverable schedules, conduct design reviews, oversee vendor technical compliance, and verify quality standards from initial feasibility through plant commissioning.
