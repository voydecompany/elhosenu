ENGINEERING PROFILE

PRECISION-DRIVEN METHODOLOGY.

Flawless engineering begins with uncompromised field data. ElHosenu executes and processes land surveys, FMB documentation and topographical surveys to provide an accurate physical baseline for solar development.

Using high-precision total stations, GNSS RTK rovers and aerial LiDAR/drone surveys, our surveyors establish precise land boundaries, boundary stone positions, and elevation contours. We cross-verify Field Measurement Book (FMB) sketches against physical ground conditions to avoid land disputes and costly design revisions.

TECHNICAL RIGOR

Technical accuracy eliminates site downtime and installation revisions. Every engineering package we generate rigorously addresses physical, environmental and statutory factors:

    Integration with national and regional coordinate reference systems (UTM/WGS84)
  • ✓
    Identification of easements, right-of-way (ROW), water bodies and high-tension lines
