ENGINEERING PROFILE

PRECISION-DRIVEN METHODOLOGY.

A solar power facility is first and foremost a structural asset. ElHosenu delivers civil design and structural analysis for mounting systems, foundations, substations and site works engineered to withstand extreme environmental forces.

Our licensed civil and structural engineers utilize advanced finite element analysis (FEA) and structural code calculation software (STAAD.Pro, SAP2000) to evaluate dead, live, wind, and seismic loads. We design driven steel piles, ground screws, concrete ballast blocks, and custom rooftop support frames that satisfy international building standards.

TECHNICAL RIGOR

KEY ENGINEERING CONSIDERATIONS.

Technical accuracy eliminates site downtime and installation revisions. Every engineering package we generate rigorously addresses physical, environmental and statutory factors:

  • ✓
    Aerodynamic uplift, vortex shedding and torsional flutter under typhoon winds
  • ✓
    Soil corrosivity and sacrificial steel zinc galvanization thickness
  • ✓
    Differential settlement across soft or heterogeneous ground strata
  • ✓
    Structural safety factors, deflection limits and connection hardware shear
