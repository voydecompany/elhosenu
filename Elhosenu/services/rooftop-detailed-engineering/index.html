ENGINEERING PROFILE

PRECISION-DRIVEN METHODOLOGY.

Commercial and industrial rooftop solar systems demand absolute precision. ElHosenu prepares detailed engineering packages that harmonize electrical yield with roof geometry, structural capacity and safety clearances.

We evaluate roof decking types, purlin spacings, load paths and obstruction envelopes to craft buildable designs. Our packages define string configurations, DC routing pathways, combiner box locations, inverter staging and AC grid tie-in points, preventing costly site clashes and warranty issues.

TECHNICAL RIGOR

KEY ENGINEERING CONSIDERATIONS.

Technical accuracy eliminates site downtime and installation revisions. Every engineering package we generate rigorously addresses physical, environmental and statutory factors:

  • ✓
    Dead and live roof loading capacity under maximum module burden
  • ✓
    Puck-to-purlin fastener pullout and shear resistance
  • ✓
    Thermal expansion joints along continuous aluminum mounting rails
  • ✓
    IBC, NFPA 70 (NEC 690), and local fire safety setbacks
