ENGINEERING PROFILE

PRECISION-DRIVEN METHODOLOGY.

Ground-mounted solar developments depend on how intelligently the design responds to topography and geotechnical conditions. ElHosenu develops optimized array layouts that minimize earthworks while maximizing generational yield.

From fixed-tilt racks to single-axis tracker corridors, our engineers align table configurations with undulating land contours. We establish table pitch, ground coverage ratios (GCR), DC trenching routes, internal haul roads, perimeter fencing, and inverter stations for seamless field construction.
